description: How status updates are written during an incident.
applies_to: [incident-triage]
---
Status updates follow this shape, in this order: what users see (impact), what we know (facts only), what we are doing (current step), next update time. No blame, no speculation stated as fact, no internal hostnames. Severity vocabulary: SEV1 (customer-facing outage), SEV2 (degraded), SEV3 (internal only).
